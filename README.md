One image summing up Friday 20 November 2026's world news: 38 stories from 7 regions.

This version rebuilds the page around structure, not decoration: a "Friday in Order" timeline strip runs the day from the overnight Kyiv strikes to COP31's last day with no deal; the three heroes (Kyiv's bridges, Cyclone Akvan, the AI sell-off) carry their key numbers inside the picture; every one of the eleven smaller stories wears a red banner with its core fact and a source chip on its picture, so each story reads from its picture alone; headlines are cut to seven words or fewer; and a smaller world map with one pin per story sits beside a wider "Also on Friday" list of 24 more stories, above a DATE CHECK box showing what was left out as Thursday news or not-yet-happened.

`main.ts` (Node 22) copies the pre-rendered `entry.png` (1024×1024, drawn deterministically with Pillow by `draw.py`) to the output directory. Run it twice and the output is byte-identical.
